    How to play

    Every level starts in the workshop. Bolt parts onto the blue grid to make a vehicle, press GO, and get the pig across the finish line. Each level has three stars: finish, grab the golden star, and beat the clock.

    Building

    Click / drag
    place parts (drag paints a whole row)
    Right-click
    remove a part
    Drag empty space
    look around (wheel zooms)
    Q E
    turn the view
    1–9
    pick a part
    I
    inside boxes: drop the pig, engine or TNT into a frame
    M
    mirror: copy to the other side
    R
    turn rockets and propellers
    X
    eraser (drag to erase lots)
    Ctrl+Z
    undo
    Enter
    GO

    Driving

    W S
    forward and reverse (needs an engine)
    A D
    steer
    Shift
    brake
    Space
    fire soda rockets
    F
    propellers on or off
    Flying
    with propellers on, W/S give more or less power and A/D steer in the air. Point propellers Up (R while building) to make a helicopter.
    B
    pop a balloon
    T
    detonate TNT
    C
    chase or side camera
    P
    ray-traced photo
    R / Esc
    restart / back to building

    Wheels bolt on with an axle to whatever box they touch, and only drive when that chunk has an engine. Parts inside a box are protected by it. Parts that take a big hit snap off.
